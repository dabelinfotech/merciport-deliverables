# Merciport Customer User Manual
**Version:** 1.0 | **Role:** End-User / Customer

## 1. Getting Started
### Registration & Login
1. Download the Merciport App from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Open the app and select **Sign Up**.
3. Enter your email, full name, and create a secure password.
4. Log in using your credentials to access your personalized Impact Dashboard.

## 2. Discovering Eco-Suppliers
1. Navigate to the **Discover** tab.
2. Browse the list of verified suppliers (e.g., Renewable Energy, Waste Management).
3. Tap on a supplier to view their profile and available services.

## 3. Booking a Service
1. Select the desired service from the supplier's list.
2. Enter the **Quantity** or number of units you wish to purchase.
3. Tap **Confirm Booking**.
4. Once confirmed, your transaction is processed, and the carbon savings are calculated.

## 4. Tracking Your Impact
1. Go to the **Impact Dashboard**.
2. View your **Total Carbon Saved** (measured in tonnes of CO2e).
3. Review your transaction history to see how each choice contributed to the planet.
