# Merciport Supplier User Manual
**Version:** 1.0 | **Role:** Service Provider / Supplier Admin

## 1. Onboarding
### Creating Your Profile
1. Register an account and select the **Supplier Admin** role.
2. Complete your business profile including Business Name, Category, and Location.
3. Submit your profile for verification. You will be notified once the Merciport Admin verifies your business.

## 2. Managing Your Services
### Adding a New Service
1. Navigate to the **Service Management** page.
2. Click **Add Service**.
3. Enter the Service Name, Price, and most importantly, the **Carbon Offset per Unit**.
4. Save the service to make it visible to customers.

## 3. Monitoring Impact
1. Access your **Supplier Dashboard**.
2. Track the total amount of CO2 your business has helped offset through customer bookings.
3. Monitor your total order volume to analyze business growth.
