# Merciport Platform - UAT Scenarios
**Target:** Stakeholder Sign-off (Milestone M9)

## Scenario 1: The Customer Journey
**Persona:** Eco-conscious Consumer
- **Step 1:** Register an account and log in.
- **Step 2:** Search for a "Renewable Energy" supplier.
- **Step 3:** Book a service (quantity: 2).
- **Step 4:** Verify that the "Your Impact" dashboard updates immediately with the correct CO2 saved.
- **Acceptance:** User confirms the flow is intuitive and the carbon value is visible.

## Scenario 2: The Supplier Journey
**Persona:** Eco-Service Provider
- **Step 1:** Log in to the Supplier Portal.
- **Step 2:** Add a new service with a specific carbon offset coefficient.
- **Step 3:** Check if the service appears in the Customer App.
- **Step 4:** View total impact generated from customer bookings.
- **Acceptance:** Supplier confirms services are managed correctly and impact is tracked.

## Scenario 3: The Admin Journey
**Persona:** Merciport Operator
- **Step 1:** Log in to the Command Centre.
- **Step 2:** Review a pending supplier application.
- **Step 3:** Verify the supplier.
- **Step 4:** Confirm that the supplier is now visible to customers.
- **Acceptance:** Admin confirms verification process is efficient.
